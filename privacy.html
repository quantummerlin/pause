Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Overview

The Gravity Pause experiment is designed with privacy as a core principle. We collect minimal data and never sell or share personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

What we collect

Anonymous usage data: We collect aggregated, anonymous information about how people use this site, including page views, session duration, and return visit patterns. This data cannot be used to identify individual users.

Local storage: If you enter a birthdate, it may be stored locally on your device (not on our servers) to improve your experience on return visits. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

No personal identification: We do not collect names, email addresses, or any information that could identify you personally.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses minimal cookies necessary for basic functionality. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

If advertisements are displayed on this site, they may use cookies as governed by the advertising provider's privacy policy (such as Google AdSense). You can opt out of personalized advertising through your browser settings or the advertising provider's opt-out mechanisms.

Third-party services

This site may use the following third-party services:

  • Analytics services for anonymous usage statistics
  • Advertising services (Google AdSense) for displaying non-intrusive advertisements
  • Content delivery networks for faster page loading

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing their data practices.

Data retention

Anonymous usage data is retained for analytical purposes. No personal data is retained because no personal data is collected.

Your rights

Since we don't collect personal data, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete. Local storage on your device can be cleared through your browser settings at any time.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to the Quantum Merlin team.
