What is the Start of Day pause?

The Start of Day pause marks the transition from night into the active hours. It occurs at 6:00 AM local time — a threshold that many traditions recognize as the beginning of the day's true activity.

This moment carries a particular quality: the day ahead is still unformed. Decisions haven't been made, reactions haven't occurred, the day's story hasn't been written. For a brief window, possibility feels more available than it will later.

Noticing this moment doesn't require waking at 6 AM. The pause exists whether you're awake for it or not. But for those who do notice it, there's often a sense of alignment — a feeling of starting alongside something larger than personal routine.
