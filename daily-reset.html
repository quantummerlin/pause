What is the Daily Reset?

The Daily Reset is the moment when one day transitions into the next. It occurs at local midnight for each person, creating a rolling wave of renewal that circles the globe continuously.

Unlike the global Gravity Pause which happens simultaneously for everyone, the Daily Reset is personal to your location. When your day ends, someone else's is just beginning. This creates a continuous thread of transition that never stops.

Many people find that consciously noting this threshold — even briefly — creates a sense of completion. The day, whatever it held, is now behind you. What comes next is unwritten.
