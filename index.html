What is the Gravity Pause?

The Gravity Pause is a daily shared moment that occurs at the same instant for everyone around the world. It marks a point in time where nothing is required — a brief threshold between one moment and the next.

Unlike calendars or clocks that divide time into tasks and obligations, the Gravity Pause simply exists. It doesn't ask you to do anything, believe anything, or change anything. It's a marker, nothing more.

Many people find that noticing these moments — even briefly — creates a subtle sense of connection to something larger than their immediate circumstances. Others simply appreciate having a fixed point in time that isn't attached to productivity or purpose.

The Gravity Pause occurs daily at UTC midnight, which means it arrives at different local times depending on where you are. This shared timing creates a quiet thread connecting people across time zones, cultures, and circumstances.
