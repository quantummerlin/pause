What is the Before Sleep pause?

The Before Sleep pause marks the transition from the day's activity into the quieter hours before rest. It occurs at 10:00 PM local time — a threshold that many cultures have recognized as the beginning of the night's stillness.

This isn't about sleep schedules or productivity. It's simply a marker of a natural transition that happens whether we notice it or not. The world doesn't stop at this hour, but something in the rhythm of the day does shift.

Many people find that acknowledging this moment — even briefly — helps the mind begin its natural process of unwinding. Not through effort, but through recognition.
